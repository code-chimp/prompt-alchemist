Clean lines, generous whitespace, focus on content over chrome. Emphasizes breathing room and clarity.

Key Characteristics

  • Generous padding (24-32px)
  • Minimal borders and dividers
  • Large touch targets
  • Clean typography hierarchy

Best For

Users who prefer calm, uncluttered interfaces. Prioritizes focus and reduces visual noise. Works well on larger displays (1440px+).

Trade-offs

Less information density means more scrolling. May feel too sparse for power users who want maximum content per screen.
